# VR Gestures

NomaiVR replaces some buttons with motion controls. They work the same on every controller. For the buttons, see the input prompts on your hand, or [Steam Frame Controls](STEAM_FRAME_CONTROLS.md) if you use Steam Frame controllers.

## Gestures

| Gesture | What it does |
|---|---|
| Touch the side of your head (helmet) with either hand | Toggle the flashlight. While your hand stays there, pressing grip toggles it again. The gesture can be turned off with **Flashlight gesture** in the mod settings. |
| Reach to your tool belt and hold grip | Take a tool. Release grip to put it back. See [Tool belt](#tool-belt). |
| Bring a marshmallow to your mouth | Eat it. |
| Touch the stick tip with the fresh marshmallow in your other hand | Put a new marshmallow on the roasting stick. The fresh one appears once the stick is empty. |
| Press the translator's buttons with the index finger of your other hand | Use the translator's on-device buttons. |
| Look at your dominant hand | Wake up at the start of a loop. |

Gesture tutorials appear when they're relevant, e.g. near Nomai text, at scout prompts, or in the dark until you've used the flashlight gesture. They can be turned off with **VR gesture tutorials** in the mod settings.

## Tool belt

The tools hang on a belt below your head:

| Tool | Belt slot |
|---|---|
| Signalscope | Right |
| Scout launcher | Middle (in front of you) |
| Translator | Left |

Reach to the slot, **hold grip** to take the tool, and **release grip** to put it back. **Toolbelt Height** in the mod settings moves the belt up or down, and **Hide toolbelt if hand is far** hides it until your hand is near.

## Laser pointer

The laser points from your dominant hand. While you hold a tool, it moves to the hand holding the tool.

Point the laser at something and press the interact button to talk, pick things up or use them. Menus work the same way with the select button.

In the ship, the cockpit screens and buttons react to the laser as well: the **scout screen**, **signalscope screen**, **landing camera screen** and the **autopilot button**.

## Left-handed players

The **Left Hand Dominant** mod setting moves the laser to your left hand. Held tools and the roasting stick default to the left hand too. The buttons stay the same.
